03 / Story

The enemy remembers every cell

During the height of the Cold War, an expedition discovers the remains of an ancient underwater civilization surrounding an unidentified lifeform. After recovery, the specimen mutates under the influence of Morticaius and rapidly escapes containment.

As facilities collapse across the country, the newly formed B.L.A.C.K.O.U.T. Unit is deployed against an organism capable of consuming, replacing, and rebuilding living hosts from only a handful of surviving cells.
