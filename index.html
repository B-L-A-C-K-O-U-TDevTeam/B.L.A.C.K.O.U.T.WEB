Situation Report

Summary of the containment crisis, player role, and primary mission mandate.

Following the discovery of an ancient civilization beneath the ocean, an unknown biological organism known as Morticaius escapes containment and begins transforming living creatures into adaptive predators.

You are the newest member of the elite B.L.A.C.K.O.U.T. Unit. Working alongside story-driven AI squadmates, you must contain the outbreak, rescue survivors, recover critical research, and uncover the truth behind what was found beneath the sea.

Every deployment pushes the unit deeper into a world of Cold War secrecy, collapsing facilities, and biological threats capable of rebuilding themselves from only a handful of surviving cells.

Core Features

The experience rewards preparation, controlled engagements, squad coordination, and resource discipline.

F-01

Methodical Tactical Combat

Deliberate first-person gunplay built around positioning, realistic weapon handling, planning, and controlled engagements.

F-02

AI Squad Companions

Story-driven allies support coordinated assaults, containment operations, research recovery, and survivor rescue missions.

F-03

Cold War Science Fiction

Authentic 1970s military influence is fused with experimental equipment and grounded retro-futuristic technology.

F-04

Adaptive Biological Threats

Morticaius creates evolving predators with regeneration rules, tactical weak points, and changing combat behaviors.

F-05

Story-Driven Missions

Operations unfold across secure laboratories, military bases, quarantined towns, and ancient underwater ruins.

F-06

Research Progression

Recover classified research to understand the infection, expose the buried truth, and advance the campaign.

F-07

Funding and Equipment

Successful missions provide funding for weapons, facilities, squad equipment, and experimental technology.

F-08

Resource Management

Balance ammunition, medical supplies, squad readiness, and mission risk before committing to each deployment.

Incident History

A discovery beneath the ocean becomes humanity's greatest biological containment crisis.

Phase 01

The Discovery

An expedition locates the remains of an ancient underwater civilization and recovers an unidentified lifeform.

Phase 02

Containment Failure

The Morticaius organism escapes study, consumes living hosts, and spreads through secured research facilities.

Phase 03

Unit Activation

The B.L.A.C.K.O.U.T. Unit deploys to stabilize affected zones, save survivors, and secure vital evidence.

Phase 04

Return to the Depths

The campaign leads back beneath the sea, where the origin of the organism and the lost civilization await.
