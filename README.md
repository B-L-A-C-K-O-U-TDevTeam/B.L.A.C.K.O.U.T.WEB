B.L.A.C.K.O.U.T.

Overview

B.L.A.C.K.O.U.T. is a single-player tactical first-person shooter that blends 1970s Cold War, science fiction, and survival horror. Following the discovery of an ancient underwater civilization, the Morticaius infection escapes containment, forcing an elite government unit to prevent humanity’s extinction.

Players command the B.L.A.C.K.O.U.T. Unit alongside AI squad companions to contain the outbreak, rescue survivors, recover classified research, and uncover the truth behind the mysterious civilization hidden beneath the sea.



Core Features

* Tactical first-person combat focused on realism and planning.
* Story-driven campaign with AI squad companions.
* 1970s Cold War setting fused with retro-futuristic technology.
* Dynamic science-fiction survival horror.
* Mission-based progression with exploration and environmental storytelling.
* Recover research logs to uncover the origins of Morticaius.
* Funding system that unlocks new weapons, equipment, facilities, and experimental technology.
* Unique enemy biology requiring tactical engagement rather than traditional zombie gameplay.

Story

During the height of the Cold War, an expedition discovers the remains of an ancient underwater civilization surrounding an unidentified lifeform. Shortly after being recovered, the specimen mutates under the influence of an unknown biological organism known as Morticaius. The infection rapidly escapes containment, consuming and replacing living hosts while evolving into increasingly dangerous forms. With research facilities collapsing across the country, the newly formed B.L.A.C.K.O.U.T. Unit is deployed to contain the outbreak before humanity loses the war against an enemy that can rebuild itself from only a handful of surviving cells.



Project Vision

B.L.A.C.K.O.U.T. aims to deliver a grounded tactical shooter where preparation, teamwork, and intelligent decision-making matter more than constant action. The game combines realistic combat, atmospheric horror, and science-fiction mystery into a campaign where every mission expands the player’s understanding of the Morticaius outbreak.
